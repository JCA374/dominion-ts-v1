The data

A teacher question is a position as the advisor reads it off the Steam screen, and nothing more: the supply pile sizes, my deck split into draw pile, hand, discard and play area, the opponent's cards (only how many are where: which ones are in their hand is unknown), turns played, and for a buy the coins, buys and actions left. Example (turn 1, $5): supply Copper 46, Silver 40, … Witch 10; my hand Copper×2, Estate×3; in play Copper×5; the opponent's ten starting cards unseen.

What the teacher may not see

The order of my draw pile and everything hidden about the opponent. Each draw deals those cards again: my unseen cards are shuffled into my draw pile; the opponent's deck, hand and discard are pooled and dealt again at the same sizes (the public sampler, obs 1, the same view the advisor has). Cards the rules make public, such as a Bureaucrat's Victory card on top of a deck, stay where they are. bv-v1's tests check that two positions that differ only in hidden cards get identical grades.

The candidates

Buy: every legal buy if there are at most 8; otherwise the net's pick, passing, and c2's five next-best buys. Action: every action card in hand and "end the action phase". Each candidate is played on every draw.

Where the test positions come from

Matches: c2 with the teacher plays frozen c2 on 300 kingdoms from the seeded stream ts1 (random 10-card kingdoms of the base set), both seats each. Every arm plays the same kingdoms and starting seeds, so arms are paired game by game. No arm sees its test kingdoms beforehand, and nothing is trained here: the only "fitting" is choosing settings, which is why the final pick is confirmed on a second stream.
